Who we are giving to

Children who live and work on the streets of our area, some of them sorting through garbage to help their families. Christmas usually passes them by.

How it works

Our team buys the gifts and hands each one to a child in person. We work from a list of families that our partner church gives us, and we keep their names private.

After Christmas, every donor receives photos from the distribution and a short report on how the gifts were given.

Who we are

People in Need is a small ministry in Zahle, Lebanon. Since April 2021 we have given direct aid to displaced Syrian and Lebanese families, and we have served more than 500 families. We serve everyone, whatever their religion, race or background, and we work together with local Christian churches. Our mission is to help, serve and bless as the hands of Jesus.

Before major holidays our volunteers deliver food baskets to families in need.
